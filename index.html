Net Worth
--
Current balance — everything you own minus everything you owe
Net worth = Total Assets − Total Debt. Refreshed each time the ledger updates. Net Worth = Total Assets − Total Debt Change since 8 September 2026. That is the first day every figure was measured the same way (euro assets converted at the live rate, loans at your share). The change is split in two: re-valuations (you or a price index changed what the house or a vehicle is worth) and everything else (market moves, money saved, debt repaid, vehicle wear). Why there is no year-to-date figure: the 1 January value came from the old spreadsheet, which valued the house and the loans differently. Comparing today with it showed a gain that was really a change of method. $ / € switch (top right): most of your net worth is in euros. Shown in dollars, it moves whenever the exchange rate moves, even if nothing changed for you. The euro view removes that.

Velocity & Momentum
--
 
 
Velocity & Momentum — how fast your net worth has been changing recently. The window: the last 90 days, but never starting earlier than 8 September 2026. Before that the figures were still being corrected (accounts added, loans restated to your share, euro assets re-converted), so counting them would show "growth" that was really bookkeeping. What is left out: any day you (or a price index) changed what the house or a vehicle is worth. That is a re-valuation, not something that repeats every month, so it is listed under the figure instead of being spread into a monthly pace. What is still inside: market moves (mostly BTC), money saved, debt repaid and vehicle wear. There is no deposit ledger to separate them, so read it as "how much richer on paper", not "investment return". In the dollar view it also contains the euro's move against the dollar; the card says how much. Pace: the window change spread evenly per month. It describes the period just gone; it is not multiplied out to a yearly figure because most of it is market movement. Momentum compares the last 30 days with the whole window: "Speeding up" or "Slowing" if the last 30 days differ by more than 10%, otherwise "Steady". Projected end of year / 1 year / 5 years come from the same engine as the FIRE card and the Scenario Lab, applied to your whole net worth: bank loans follow their schedule, your monthly new money and today's rates continue, vehicles lose value, and BTC and the house either stay at today's value or keep their past growth (the switch on the FIRE card). They are no longer a straight line through the recent pace. Old-method notice: the newer recent-pace calculation is not available from the database yet, so this card is temporarily showing the old year-to-date figure. It counts from 1 January (a hand-typed starting value) and includes set-up week, so it overstates growth.

Market · Bitcoin

always portfolio-wide — BTC is BTC in every scope
BTC
-- BTC @ --
Bitcoin module. Holdings and spot price come from the BTC trackers. Your average buy price is worked out from your recorded purchases and sales (each buy at the BTC price that day; a sale leaves the average unchanged). Entries marked "estimate" are periods the blockchain cannot show, priced at that period's average. BTC that built up inside liquidity positions has no single purchase date; it is dated and priced on the day it reached your loan. Your return on the BTC you hold = what it is worth now minus what you paid for the BTC you still have. It is a paper result: nothing is gained or lost until you sell. What your past sales gained or lost is given separately in the note under the figures. In the euro view the amount is converted at today's rate. BTC price, year to date is the market's move since 1 January, not your own result.

Planning

always portfolio-wide — projections are never re-scoped
FIRE Projection
Where your investable pot is heading, and when it reaches your target. The pot counts investment assets only: BTC, stablecoin positions and funds, minus the Aave loan. Real estate and vehicles are never part of it (they still count toward Net Worth). How the line is built: month by month from what you hold today. Bank loans follow their schedule, your monthly new money keeps coming, the stablecoin positions earn today's rates and the Aave loan costs today's rate. A loan that ends frees its payment, which is then invested. If nothing changes holds BTC at today's price. Past growth continues lets BTC keep the yearly rate it has had since tracking began in Aug 2023, an optimistic reading. Nobody knows the BTC price, so two more lines show it doing badly and doing well: the 1-in-10 outcomes on either side, sized from how much BTC has really moved since 2023. No probabilities, no random runs: the same inputs always give the same answer, on every device. Every input and its source is listed in the Scenario Lab, where you can also test changes.
Milestones · to $1M FIRE
One milestone tracker — and this is it. Progress is measured on the investable nest egg (investment assets — the same basis as the FIRE projection), so "$1M" means the same thing everywhere. Real estate & vehicles are excluded. Net worth itself has no milestone bar: mixing a house into a FIRE milestone would fake the finish line. The dates are read off the same line as the FIRE card: loan schedules, your monthly new money and today's rates, with BTC either held at today's price or keeping its past growth (the switch on the FIRE card). The status line also gives the date if BTC does well or badly. Shown in dollars.
