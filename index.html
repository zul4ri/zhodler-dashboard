--
 
Net Worth Velocity — how fast your wealth is compounding right now. It turns the year-to-date gain into an annual rate so this year can be compared fairly against any other. Annualized rate = YTD return × 12 ÷ months elapsed Momentum = YTD monthly pace minus all-time monthly pace. FIRE Projection
Monte Carlo runs (0 = off)2,000
Results are saved and stay on screen until you run a new projection.
FIRE probability = the share of simulated futures where the investable nest egg reaches your FIRE number. The nest egg counts investment assets only — real estate and vehicles are never part of the FIRE target (they still count toward Net Worth). Click ⚙ to see every parameter and its source. Runs: 0 (off) to 20,000 — more runs = smoother bands, slower calculation Results persist (saved with your dashboard) until you calculate a new projection — reloading shows the last run, never a blank.
